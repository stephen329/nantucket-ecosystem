## Why this is a proposal and not a transcription

The owner asked for §4 to be transcribed. **§4 contains no matrix to
transcribe.** `docs/strategy/nantucket-ecosystem-integrated-strategy.md:124-154`
is four prose sections of brand handoff rules plus a five-line *Global
communication rule*, and that rule is the source of the one-sender invariant the
matrix states. It enumerates no message classes. The six rows in the working
skeleton were derived from it by someone; they are not quoted from it.

So producing the marketing rows is **authorship, not transcription**, and
authorship of the class taxonomy is what the marker holds for a human. This
document is the draft he asked for, in the only form that does not fill the
marker: a proposal carrying his approval block, operative on his signature and
not before.

## The proposed families

A **family** is one customer job that more than one brand does, **under one
consent purpose**. Its **members** are the per-brand classes, one per brand, each
with exactly one sending brand — which is what keeps the routing invariant true
by construction. The widening carries a grant along a family, resolved to the
recipient brand's member, and it must preserve the purpose.

**The purposes are the strategy's, not this document's — corrected after review
(P2), and it corrects something this document previously got wrong.** §5 of
`docs/strategy/nantucket-ecosystem-integrated-strategy.md` lists the required
consent states, and two of them are marketing:

<!-- law: docs/strategy/nantucket-ecosystem-integrated-strategy.md -->
> - Rental marketing opt-in
> - Property-interest opt-in or explicit inquiry

*An earlier round recorded that "no purpose vocabulary exists anywhere in the
records to derive from," and declined to assign purposes on that ground. **That
was wrong.** §5 supplies one for exactly the classes this instrument proposes, and
the honest correction is that it was not looked for in the ratified strategy
before the absence was asserted.*

**A single `property-showcase` family was the consequence of that error and is
now split.** It had NantucketRentals.com showing rental homes to renters and
Congdon & Coleman showing homes for sale to buyers — **two purposes in one
family**, so a rental grant would have resolved to a for-sale send. That is the
purpose axis moving under cover of a brand resolution, which the widening clause
forbids as plainly as it forbids the class axis moving.

were put to him, the provenance option was described as needing *"the surface

`communication_consents` carries **`consent_version` (NOT NULL)** and **`source`**,

**His answer stands as given, and whether that correction changes it is his.** It
is not narrowed here on a contributor's reading: he was asked what an existing
grant permits and he said both, and a drafter trimming that to "both, except where

the safer direction. **What is recorded is that the option set was misdescribed
when he chose from it**, and that `consent_version` and `source` are the evidence
a narrower rule would run on if he wants one.

**Why "both" is the faithful reading and not the permissive one.** A drafter first
described this option as letting a grant reach *"a class the person was never
shown"* — which reads as scope creep and is the wrong description. The person was
shown, and agreed to, **`rental_marketing`**. They were shown no class at all,
because the class axis did not exist when they granted it. So "both" is **the
whole of the purpose they actually granted**, and the two narrower readings are
the ones that would change their grant: assigning it to one class would silence
mail they consented to on the strength of a distinction drawn afterwards, and
treating it as neither would void the grant outright and ask them again for
something they had already given.

**What it is scoped to.** Grants collected **before** the classes exist. His
answer is retroactive relief for rows that predate the split, and he was asked
about exactly those.

**What that leaves unsolved, and an earlier version of this paragraph denied it
existed — found after review (P2).** It said *"a grant collected once the taxonomy
is live carries its own class and needs no rule."* **False, for as long as
collection keeps using the ratified label.** That label offers *"news, featured
listings, and special offers"* in one checkbox — and under this taxonomy
**featured listings is `rental-showcase` and special offers is `rental-offer`**,
two classes under one `rental_marketing` purpose. So a **new** grant is no more
self-classifying than an old one, and his *"both families"* rule does not reach it
because it is scoped, by its own terms, to grants collected before the classes
exist.

**Left unaddressed, phase 7 must guess** — and the two wrong guesses are the ones
his answer already rejected for legacy rows: recording one class discards mail the
person agreed to, and recording neither voids the grant.

**Proposed: the collector emits a record for each concrete per-brand class the
collected copy covers, on each channel it names.** The table below enumerates them
for the ratified label as it stands. This is the same shape as his answer for
legacy rows, applied at collection instead of retroactively, and it is the only
option that needs **no new customer-facing copy**. *Strike it and write the
alternative if he prefers one.*

**Members, not families — corrected after review (P2), which caught this proposal
saying "both `rental-showcase` and `rental-offer`."** `rental-showcase` is a
**family**, not a class: its members are the NantucketRentals.com and Nantucket

family/class conflation that a review caught once already in this document, in the

**And each record is one channel, not four.** `communication_consents.channel`

contributor cannot narrow ratified wording by storing less than it offers.

precisely so the person can separate them later.
