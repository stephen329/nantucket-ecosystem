# Congdon & Coleman — Photography Specification (Listing & Advisory Surfaces)

Destination: `assets/congdon-coleman/photography-spec.md`
Status: DRAFT 2026-08-12 for Kristy's sign-off. Directions recorded from Stephen; derived rules marked (D) and may be struck or amended at sign-off.
Scope: listing photography and all imagery on advisory/valuation documents and congdonandcoleman.com listing surfaces. **Brand photography (advisor portraits, office, general island imagery) is explicitly NOT governed by this spec** (Stephen, 2026-08-12) — it follows the general brand guide only.

## Principle

Documentary restraint. The photograph is evidence of the property as it is: accurate light, accurate space, accurate condition. A buyer who visits after seeing the photos should find the photos truthful.

## Prohibitions (ratified by Stephen, 2026-08-12)

1. No staged people or models in listing photography.
2. No props brought to the shoot. The property is photographed as furnished and kept by its owner.

## Prohibitions derived from "accurate light" (D — Kristy to confirm or strike)

3. (D) No sky replacement or compositing of elements not present at capture.
4. (D) No HDR blending or tone-mapping that produces light which did not exist in the room.
5. (D) No post-capture color grading toward a mood (e.g., artificial golden-hour warmth). White balance renders the light that was there.
6. (D) No wide-angle distortion that misrepresents room dimensions; verticals corrected, proportions honest.

## Permitted (ratified)

- Basic corrections: exposure, contrast, lens/perspective correction, sensor-dust removal, straightening. The line is correction versus enhancement — corrections make the capture more faithful to the scene; enhancements make the scene more appealing than it was.
- Aerial/drone photography, generally permitted (subject to FAA and local rules — operational compliance is the photographer's contractual responsibility).

## Operational requirements (from shared standards)

- Every image carries a rights record: source, license status, permitted brands, credit requirement, expiry.
- Listing photography is NantucketRentals.com / Congdon & Coleman material and never migrates to Hello Nantucket surfaces.
- Alt text on every published image.
