## Fixed principles (from the brand guide, non-negotiable)

1. Every report is prepared by a **named advisor** — name and role on the cover, recommendation signed by the same person.
2. **Fact and judgment are separated and marked.** Data carries sources and sample sizes; judgment is labeled as the advisor's ("scenarios, not an appraisal conclusion"; "to be confirmed by the listing advisor").
3. **Never anonymous, never syndicated.** ~~never marketing~~ — **struck 2026-09-16 by the owner**, who classified owner advisory and market reports as *"Marketing"* because it is *"the safe choice to avoid CAN-SPAM violations."* The struck words are kept so the rule they replaced stays readable. This document class is owner advisory under the Congdon & Coleman sending brand; **consent class per shared standards**, which is where his answer landed — `skill/references/shared-standards.md` now reads **Marketing (explicit opt-in)** for this row. **What the reclassification does:** it makes the consent explicit opt-in and puts these sends under marketing suppression and the marketing frequency caps — strictly more restrictive than the advisory class it replaces, which is why he chose it. **What it does not touch:** principles 1 and 2 above, and §4's requirement that representation, valuation, fiduciary judgment, negotiation or consequential advice come from a named person. A marketing consent class is not permission to send an opinion of value unsigned, anonymous, or syndicated.

## Required sections, in order

**1. Cover.** Brand mark; category line "OWNER ADVISORY · MARKET POSITIONING"; property title; subtitle; as-of date; "Prepared by [Advisor Name], [Role] · Congdon & Coleman Real Estate"; entity and license line; data attribution line ("Data provided courtesy of Congdon & Coleman Real Estate via LINK MLS. Information deemed reliable but not guaranteed.").
**E-1 RATIFIED:** valuation surfaces use the trade-name form only — no legal-entity mention. Canonical valuation entity line, verbatim: "Congdon & Coleman Real Estate, Massachusetts Licensed Real Estate Broker #422678." (The full O2554 LLC form was reserved for lease/payment surfaces when E-1 was ratified; that form was deleted 2026-09-04. E-1 itself is unaffected — valuation surfaces were already trade-name only.)

**2. Owner Executive Summary.** Subject differentiators quantified against the comparison-set median; current market snapshot (actives, pendings, median ask); a "What this means" judgment paragraph; primary strength and principal consideration, one each.

**3. Competitive Position.** Three bands — market-clearing / balanced / aspirational — each with range, $/SF, and one-sentence meaning. **Derivation disclosed in place** (anchor statistic, fixed percentages, "scenarios, not an appraisal conclusion"). Principal strengths and pricing risks. Independent value cross-checks table: indication, implied value, basis, N — with the blindness caveat (e.g., $/SF is blind to land; read alongside the assessed-value multiple).

**4. Pivotal Comparable callout.** The single sale a buyer's broker will open with, named, with the instruction that the advisor must anchor to it or explicitly distinguish it. Leaving it unaddressed concedes the comparison.

**5. Owner Takeaway** (mid-report): working band, what final positioning depends on, what buyers will compare.

**6. Subject Property Profile.** Facts with source (LINK listing ID, assessor records). Unknowns marked "to be supplied/confirmed by the listing advisor" — never silently filled.

**7. Market References.** Most-relevant closed sales and active competition with **positioning roles**; closed-sale and active-listing detail cards with "Why it matters" lines; pending status; compact table of remaining qualifying inventory.
**R-1 RATIFIED:** each comparable carries **exactly one positioning role**, used consistently everywhere it appears. (The source document labels 33 Cliff Road "most direct lower-range anchor" in one table and "strong upper-range reference" in a detail card — the template makes that a lint failure.)

**8. Pricing Position and Market Behavior.** Illustrative pricing scenarios table (labeled "analytical scenarios, not recommended list prices"); comparable-sale behavior stats (sale/list ratio, DOM, reduction frequency and depth); supply by price band with months-of-supply; owner takeaway.

**9. Market Context.** YTD/T12/T24 counts and volume with prior-period comparison.
**R-2 RATIFIED:** **missing data is never rendered as zero.** The source document's five-year chart shows 0 closed sales for 2022–2024, which a reader takes as fact; per the document's own methodology ("nothing is imputed"), unavailable years display as "n/a — outside data window," not 0. Small-sample warnings (already present for Cliff T12 n=3 and the $30M+ band) are required wherever N < 5.

**10. Feature Comparison matrix.** Attribute-by-attribute vs. subject; "Unknown" where data are unavailable.

**11. Concise Final Takeaway + Recommendation Gate.** Summary band, top references — and the gate, **RATIFIED as mandatory (E-3)**: the named advisor must record and approve the recommendation before the report is delivered to the owner. Gate text: **"[Advisor] has not yet recorded a recommended band and rationale. The report is not owner-deliverable until the recommendation is stated and signed."** No report leaves the firm with this block unresolved. The signed recommendation replaces the gate text.

**12. Appendix.** Secondary comparables; full trailing-window reference table; data-quality notes (DOM gaps, off-market flags).

**13. Methodology, Intended Use, and Limitations.** Intended use and user, effective date, not-an-appraisal scope exclusions (lending, tax, estate, litigation); data sources; rules applied (definitions of every ratio, exclusion logic, dedup logic); record treatment with explicit windows and exclusion counts.
